Anomaly

Storm VTEC minus the quiet-day mean at that station and time of day. Blue = depletion, red = enhancement (±38 TECU).

Volatility

1-hour centred rolling standard deviation of the station VTEC — how fast TEC is fluctuating right now. Blue = calm, red = turbulent (0–9 TECU).

Z-score

Anomaly divided by that station's pooled quiet-day σ. Beyond ±2σ is a genuine anomaly; the scale runs −8 to +12σ (the peak reaches +14σ, EDSV on 11 May).

RINEX → STEC → VTEC via georinex · broadcast-orbit geometry, thin-shell mapping · quiet baseline = 5 Kp-selected quiet days of May 2024 (8, 22, 25, 28, 29) · Z-score σ pooled per station over ~240 quiet-day residuals · 105 AUSCOPE/ARGN stations, Geoscience Australia.
All three panels share one diverging blue–white–red ramp (blue low, red high); ranges differ by quantity — anomaly ±38 TECU, volatility 0–9 TECU, Z-score −8 to +12σ. Times shown in UTC and AEST (UTC+10). Map canvases render as a fixed dark instrument display in both page themes.
